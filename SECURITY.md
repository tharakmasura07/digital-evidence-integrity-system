## Cryptographic Assurance & Standards Compliance

The Digital Evidence Integrity System implements cryptographic mechanisms designed to fulfill legal evidence admissibility standards:

1. **Bitstream Fingerprinting:**
   - Compliant with **FIPS 180-4 (Secure Hash Standard)**.
   - Genuine SHA-256 (256-bit) and SHA-512 (512-bit) digests computed via browser-native `crypto.subtle`.
2. **Chain of Custody Ledger:**
   - Merkle root aggregation of evidence transaction hashes.
   - Immutable block sequencing (`PreviousBlockHash` -> `CurrentBlockHash`).
   - ECDSA P-256 digital signature representation per authorized officer.
3. **Legal Admissibility:**
   - **Federal Rule of Evidence (FRE) Rule 902(14)**: Certified records generated by a process of digital identification.
   - **ISO/IEC 27037:2012**: Guidelines for identification, collection, acquisition, and preservation of digital evidence.
   - **NIST SP 800-86**: Guide to Integrating Forensic Techniques into Incident Response.
   - **CJIS Security Policy 5.9**: Criminal Justice Information Services digital storage and access controls.

## Reporting a Security Vulnerability

If you discover an issue or vulnerability that compromises the cryptographic guarantees or tamper-detection engine of DEIS, please report it responsibly:

- Do **not** open a public GitHub issue for critical security vulnerabilities.
- Provide a clear proof-of-concept demonstrating how cryptographic integrity or chain-of-custody verification is bypassed.
- Maintainers will review, verify, and address security reports promptly.
